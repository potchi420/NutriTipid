Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026. Written to meet the Data Privacy Act of 2012, Republic Act No. 10173.

Short version: everything you type stays in this browser. We do not keep a copy on a server, we do not sell it, and clearing your browser data removes it for good.

01What we collect

Only what the app needs to build a plan and show your progress:

  • Account details. Name, email address, role, and the date you joined. Your password is checked in the browser and never saved.
  • Body and goal details. Age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, and the targets computed from them.
  • Food and budget records. Meals logged, swaps accepted, pesos spent, and the micronutrient estimates built from those meals.
  • Weight entries. The date and weight you add to the weight log, plus your target weight.
  • Preferences. Light or dark mode, and the last page you opened.

We do not ask for your student number, government ID, address, phone number, or payment details, and there is no field for them.

02Where it is kept

NutriTipid runs entirely in your browser. Everything above is written to your browser's local storage on this device, under four keys: nutritipid-account, nutritipid-profile, nutritipid-weight, and nutritipid-theme.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no account database, no cloud copy, and no analytics service reading over your shoulder. If you sign in on another device or another browser, you start with an empty profile, because the data never left the first one.

Local storage is not encrypted. Anyone who can open your browser profile on this device can read it, which is why we ask you not to use the app on a shared or borrowed computer.

03Why we process it

  • To compute a weekly meal plan that fits your budget and your targets.
  • To track adherence, spending, and weight so the progress page can show a real trend.
  • To carry your theme choice and signed-in state between pages.
  • To answer you when you send feedback through the contact form.
  • To study, in aggregate and without names, whether the planner actually helps students eat better. Only the summary numbers you choose to send us are used for this.

We do not profile you for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions that affect your rights.

04Legal bases under the Data Privacy Act

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) requires a lawful basis before personal data is processed. For NutriTipid:

  • Consent. You tick the box at sign-up and you may withdraw at any time by deleting your data. Consent is the basis for storing your profile, meal records, and weight entries.
  • Explicit consent for sensitive personal information. Weight, body measurements, and any health detail you enter count as sensitive personal information under Section 3 of the Act. We process them only because you chose to enter them, and only to compute your plan and show your trend.
  • Legitimate interest. Keeping the app working, secure, and free of abuse, and improving the planner for the pilot.

Under Section 16 of the Act, you may object to processing, and under Section 17 you may ask for a copy of your data. Section 08 below explains how.

05Who sees it

Nobody outside your device, unless you send it yourself.

  • No selling, renting, or trading of personal data. Ever.
  • No advertising networks, tracking pixels, or third party analytics.
  • Web fonts are served from this site, not from an outside font service, so no request leaves for them.
  • The Google button on the login and sign-up pages is decorative for now. Pressing it sends nothing and creates no Google session.
  • If you message the team through the contact form, the team reads that message. Nothing else is attached to it.
  • If the university clinic pilot enrolls you, clinic staff see only what you agree to share in person or in writing, not your browser data.

06How long we keep it

Your data stays in your browser until you delete it. Clearing site data, using a private window, or resetting the browser removes it completely, and there is no copy left for us to recover.

Messages you send the team are kept only long enough to answer them and are deleted after that, unless you ask us to keep the thread.

07Your rights as a data subject

Under Republic Act No. 10173 you have the right to:

  • Be informed whether your data is being processed, and how.
  • Object to processing, including processing for the pilot study.
  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Rectify an error in it.
  • Erase or block it, subject to legal and contractual limits.
  • Data portability, meaning a copy in a common, readable format.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
  • Be indemnified for damages caused by a breach of the Act.

Because the data lives in your browser, most of these rights are already in your hands: edit any field on the profile page, and delete everything by clearing site data.

08Exercising your rights

For access, correction, portability, or erasure of anything you cannot reach from the profile page, contact the Data Protection Officer through the contact form and mark the message Data Privacy.

We reply within fifteen working days. We may ask a question or two to confirm the request is really yours, but we will never ask for your password.

09Security

NutriTipid keeps data local on purpose: nothing to breach on a server, nothing to leak in transit. The tradeoff is that the security of your data depends on this device.

  • Use a password you do not reuse on other sites.
  • Do not use the app on a shared computer, or clear site data after you do.
  • Keep your device and browser updated.
  • Log out before handing the device to someone else.

If you believe your data was exposed, contact the DPO right away so the team can help and, where the Act requires it, notify the National Privacy Commission.

10Minors

The pilot is built for college students, most of whom are eighteen or older. If you are below eighteen, use NutriTipid only with a parent or guardian and with their consent.

We do not knowingly process a child's personal data without that consent. If a guardian asks us to remove a minor's data, we will help them erase it from the device.

11Cookies and local storage

No tracking cookies. The app uses browser local storage, which works like a cookie but is never sent to a server. It holds your theme choice, your signed-in name, your profile, and your weight log.

To clear it in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox: open settings, find site data for this site, and delete it. On a phone, clearing browsing data for the browser does the same. The app will open empty and you can start again.

12Changes to this policy

When the way NutriTipid handles data changes, this page changes first. The date at the top always shows the current version, and a material change is announced on the dashboard.

The Terms of Service cover the rest of the agreement between you and the project.

13Contact and complaints

Data Protection Officer, NutriTipid. Reach the DPO through the contact form and mark the message Data Privacy.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission, 3rd Floor Delegation Building, PICC Complex, Roxas Boulevard, Pasay City. The commission accepts complaints through privacy.gov.ph and complaints@privacy.gov.ph.
