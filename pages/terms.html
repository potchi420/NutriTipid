01About these terms

NutriTipid is a capstone project built for Filipino students living on a daily food budget. These terms cover the website, the planner, the diary, the weight log, and every page under this domain.

If you do not accept these terms, please do not create an account. If you already have one, you can stop using the app at any time and clear your data from the profile page.

02What NutriTipid does

You tell us a daily food budget, a calorie and protein target, and the meals you actually eat. NutriTipid turns that into a seven day plan, priced from local carinderia and campus canteen price ranges, and tracks how closely you followed it.

The plan is a suggestion, not a schedule you are graded on. Swaps exist because real days change: a dish runs out, a class runs late, a friend brings food.

03Your account

Use one account per person and keep the login to yourself. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

This pilot runs inside your browser, so the details you enter never leave your device. That also means a password is only checked here, not on a server. Please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Give accurate body details if you want accurate targets. A plan built on the wrong height or weight will still run, it will just fit poorly.

04Plans, prices, and numbers

Dish prices are reference values for Metro Manila canteens and carinderias. They are rounded to fives and they change with the market, the shop, and the day. Your actual receipt decides what you spent.

Calorie, protein, and micronutrient figures are estimates based on standard serving sizes and Philippine food composition references. Cooking oil, extra rice, and free soup are not measured, so treat every number as a close guide.

NutriTipid is free to use. Nothing in the app is an offer to sell food, and no price shown here is a quote from any vendor.

05Not medical advice

NutriTipid is an education and planning tool, not a clinic. Nothing here diagnoses, treats, or replaces advice from a doctor or a licensed nutritionist dietitian.

Talk to a professional before changing how you eat if you are pregnant or nursing, if you manage diabetes, hypertension, kidney disease, or an eating disorder, or if you take medication that interacts with food. If a plan ever feels wrong for your body, stop and ask for help.

If you feel faint, severely weak, or unwell while following any plan, seek medical care right away.
